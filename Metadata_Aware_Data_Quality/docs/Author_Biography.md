# Author Biography

FNU Aditya Ranjan is a U.S.-based Senior Data Engineering professional with
more than 16 years of experience in data engineering, data warehousing, data
modeling, analytics, and enterprise data platforms. His professional and
research interests include modern warehouse and lakehouse architectures,
metadata management, data quality, data governance, data lineage, semantic
data systems, and AI-ready enterprise data platforms. His work emphasizes
practical, reproducible approaches to improving the reliability, governance,
and operational effectiveness of data-intensive systems.
